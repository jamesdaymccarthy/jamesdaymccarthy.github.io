The Diggamajigger

A giant worm that lives underground in the beach. It has four fins on top and one hundred trillion legs on the bottom.

Your beach is on a round little planet. Tunnel through sand, clay, fossil rock, crystal caverns and magma, down through the mantle to the molten core, and straight out the other side. Or just walk and swim all the way around. Dig up 46 treasures, 14 secret places and 18 kinds of creature along the way. Careful: dig under the sea and the water comes pouring in.

Eat every kind of creature and find every secret place (or just type EAT) and you become a Planet Eater: every tunnel makes you bigger, until you swallow the whole planet and have to fly through space to find a new one.
