I AM DUNG

a rolling ball of destiny

You are not the beetle. You are what the beetle rolls. Absorb twigs, leaves, flowers, corn, berries, eggs and mushrooms to swell into a magnificent sphere — everything you roll up sticks to you, visibly, so watch yourself get weirder and lumpier as you grow. The rule cuts both ways: if you're bigger than something, you can roll it up. If something's bigger than you — like the fox, which starts out bigger than you are — it can and will eat you. Outgrow it and the tables turn. Chickens peck when you're small; dogs chase harder; geese hit hardest. Then rocks and trees. Then fence posts, scarecrows, silos, windmills, barns — even a tractor and a whole pond, eventually. Grab a glowing clover for a temporary magnet that pulls loose items to you. Chain pickups for a combo bonus. Storms roll through with real lightning — all bark, no bite. There's no finish line. Just see how much of the farm you can absorb.
