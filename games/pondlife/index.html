Pondlife

Live as a frog in a living pond: hunt insects with your tongue, swim, dive, dodge herons, and grow from frogspawn to a full-sized frog.

Mode
Frog
Skin pattern
Pond
How to play
WASD

Crawl. Hold Shift to hop quickly.

Mouse

Look around. Click the view to lock the mouse; scroll to zoom.

Space

Jump. Hold longer for a more powerful leap. Underwater: swim up.

Click

Shoot your tongue at the insect in the gold ring.

F

Dive under, or surface again.

E

Interact. Hold while looking at an animal to study it.

R

Croak. Other frogs may answer.

C

Switch first and third person.

Q

Sneak (lower and quieter). Underwater: swim down.

X

Rest to recover energy.

V

Insider Frog: turn yourself inside out, or turn a creature next to you inside out so it sticks to you. Telepathic Frog: take over the mind of a nearby creature. Fire Frog: breathe fire.

G

Telepathic Frog: hold to fire the telelaser. It discovers the nearest frog you have not found yet, but makes you lose a random one of your other frogs.

Z

Insider Frog: shake off everything stuck to you.

B

Multiplayer: hold to talk (when voice chat is set to push to talk).

Tab

Status, field guide, achievements, home and map.

H

Hide the interface for screenshots.

Esc

Pause and settings.

Predators give warnings: a heron coils its neck before striking, a snake rears and hisses, and a pike pushes a V-shaped wake toward you. Dive, hide in reeds, or get out of their reach. Progress saves automatically in this browser. To play offline, save this page and open the file in a modern browser.
